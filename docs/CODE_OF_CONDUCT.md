# Code of Conduct

Open-Dot-Agents is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community. We expect participants to act professionally, assume good intent,
respect differing technical opinions, and focus discussion on the work.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, unwanted
sexual attention, publishing private information, and sustained disruption
are not tolerated. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation
when behavior violates these expectations.

To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately through the security
reporting channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), marking the message as a
conduct report. Reports will be handled discreetly, with a timely
acknowledgement and proportionate action. Retaliation against a reporter is
not tolerated.
